# Credits

Onward is free and built entirely on free data and free services. Thank you to:

**Aviation data**
- **OurAirports** (public domain): airfields, runways, navaids and frequencies. https://ourairports.com
- **aviationweather.gov** (NOAA / US National Weather Service): METARs, TAFs, SIGMETs and AIRMETs.
- **The community "Microsoft Flight Simulator Map"** (Google My Maps): MSFS points of interest, photogrammetry cities and handcrafted airports. Onward downloads it from the map's public link; it isn't redistributed with the app.

**Weather and terrain**
- **Open-Meteo** (CC BY 4.0): winds aloft, the hourly forecast along the route and the flying-conditions map. https://open-meteo.com
- **MET Norway** (CC BY 4.0, api.met.no): backup forecast when Open-Meteo is busy or its free daily limit is reached. https://api.met.no
- **Terrain Tiles** on the AWS Registry of Open Data (Mapzen / Tilezen): terrain elevation. Contains SRTM, GMTED, ETOPO1 and other public sources: © USGS, NASA, NOAA, the EU-DEM (© European Environment Agency), the Canadian DEM (© Natural Resources Canada) and others. https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
- **OpenTopoData** and Open-Meteo's elevation API: terrain fallback.
- **RainViewer**: weather radar tiles. https://www.rainviewer.com
- **EUMETSAT** (EUMETView): Meteosat satellite pictures. https://view.eumetsat.int
- **NASA GIBS / Worldview**: GOES and Himawari satellite pictures. https://earthdata.nasa.gov/gibs

**Places**
- **OpenStreetMap contributors** (ODbL), via the Overpass API: landmarks. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- **Wikipedia** (CC BY-SA): short descriptions and photos of sights.

**Maps**
- OpenTopoMap (CC BY-SA), OpenStreetMap (ODbL)
- EOxCloudless by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- FAA VFR and IFR charts (US, public domain)
- open flightmaps (VFR charts), openAIP (airspace, with your own free key)

**Software**
- Leaflet (BSD-2-Clause), Python-SimConnect (AGPL-3.0), Pillow (MIT-CMU), certifi (MPL-2.0)
- Fonts: Instrument Serif, Inter Tight and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License), via Google Fonts

All data is real-world data and can differ from the sim. Charts and data are for flight simulation only, never for real-world navigation.
